Car Wash ASMR

-----

Wash two cars with one finger, enjoy cleaning sounds, and compare before & after.

-----

Turn a dirty car into a clean one, one swipe at a time.

Car Wash ASMR is a small, offline 2D car-cleaning game from Anvumi Games. Move your finger across the car and watch the dirt gradually disappear, accompanied by gentle cleaning sounds.

TWO CARS TO CLEAN
Wash a red hatchback and a blue sedan. Once a car is clean, move on to the next one or restart the current wash. After the second car, the sequence repeats.

SEE THE DIFFERENCE
After finishing a wash, hold the Before button to view the original dirt. Release it to return to the clean car.

SIMPLE CONTROLS
Play in portrait mode with one finger. Short on-screen hints explain how to clean and compare the result.

PLAY OFFLINE
No internet connection is needed to play. Turn the cleaning sound on or off whenever you like.

Your selected car and sound setting are remembered between sessions. Cleaning progress is not saved: when you reopen the game, the selected car starts with fresh dirt.

A compact game built around a simple pleasure: watching dirt disappear and seeing the result.
