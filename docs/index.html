Car Wash ASMR - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026. This policy describes Android version 1.0, version code 2.

Developer: Anvumi Games. Privacy contact: anvumi.games@gmail.com.

About the game

Car Wash ASMR is an offline car-cleaning game. It does not require an in-game account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, chat, or user-uploaded content.

Data collection and sharing during gameplay

The game does not collect personal data from gameplay or share personal data with third parties. It does not send gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, or automatic crash reports to the developer or an analytics service. Advertising, analytics, and cloud diagnostics services are not enabled in this release.

Information processed on your device

Touch or mouse input is processed locally to control cleaning. The game does not save a gesture history. The selected car and sound setting are saved locally using Unity PlayerPrefs. Partial cleaning progress is not saved. These settings are not sent to a developer server.

Opening this policy

The Privacy Policy button opens this website in your external browser. The link does not include a player identifier, saved settings, or gameplay information. Internet access is needed to load the website, but not for gameplay.

This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts, external fonts, or embedded media. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and handles website information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your browser may also process information according to its own privacy settings and policies. Website and browser processing is separate from offline gameplay.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process installation, update, device, backup, and diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. This is separate from the game's local processing. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention, deletion, and support

Local car and sound settings remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Clearing app storage resets these settings. There is no in-game account or developer-hosted gameplay profile to delete.

If you voluntarily email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you include. We use this correspondence to respond to your request, not for advertising. Email providers process correspondence to deliver the messages. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the privacy contact above, subject to those obligations. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Children and intended audience

The intended audience is teenagers aged 13 and above and adults. The game is not marketed specifically to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support correspondence, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. New features such as advertising or online services require a review of privacy practices and applicable disclosures before release. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to anvumi.games@gmail.com.
